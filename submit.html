05 Suggest an event

Know a good one? Tell us.

Anyone can suggest an event: a talk, a workshop, a volunteering day, a regular meet-up. If it's in London and welcoming to newcomers, we'd love to hear about it.

Suggest it in two minutes

Fill in a short form with the name, date, place and a link. You don't need an account.

Open the form ↗ (opens in a new tab)

What happens next

  1. Your suggestion lands in our shared sheet.
  2. Someone from the team checks the details.
  3. Once it's added, it appears on the site and the map automatically.

We look for: events in London, open to the public, and friendly to people who are new to this.
