04 We went

We went, so you know what to expect.

Going somewhere new is the hardest part. So we go first, and tell you honestly how it felt before and after.

Repair café, first time

By Team member A · Workshop · Went alone · Free

Before

“I nearly didn't go. I don't know anything about fixing things and thought everyone would be an expert.”

After
